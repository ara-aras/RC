Fly eye

This phone's camera becomes what the fly sees.
idle

Keep this tab in front, or better: go to the dashboard and tap Use this camera there — one tab, nothing to switch. A tab in the background freezes its camera and the fly would see a still picture; this page stops sending when that happens.

Mobile browsers only allow the camera on HTTPS or 127.0.0.1. Running the host on the phone: open http://127.0.0.1:8642/camera. From another device: add the origin under chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure.
